Partner with us

We work with Jobcentres, Youth Hubs, colleges, councils and community groups to reach people who would benefit most. Partners can refer learners, host in-person courses or commission places.

Fund places

Our courses are free for learners, so every place is funded by grants and supporters. A funded group gives ten to twelve people a route into tech.

Volunteer or mentor

If you work in tech, you could mentor a learner, give a talk, review portfolios or offer work experience. An hour of your time can open a door.

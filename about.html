About us

We believe that where you start shouldn't decide where you can go. Code for Change UK teaches practical coding skills to people who face barriers to education and work.

Why we exist

Tech careers can offer good pay and flexible work, including remote and part-time roles that fit around family life. But the routes in are often expensive, and they can feel out of reach for people without money, contacts or confidence.

Code for Change UK offers a different route: free, patient, practical teaching that starts from zero and ends with real work learners can show to employers.

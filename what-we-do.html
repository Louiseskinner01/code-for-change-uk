What we do

We run beginner-friendly web development courses in three lengths, so learners can start small and keep going.

Our courses

CourseLengthWhat you'll build
Short course6 weeks, plus a showcaseA responsive, interactive website, live on the internet
Medium course8 weeks, plus a showcaseA second project: an interactive JavaScript game
Full bootcamp16 weeks, plus a showcaseA full web app with Python and Django, user accounts and a database

Sessions last one to two hours and mix short explanations, live coding together and challenges at three levels, so everyone is stretched. See the full course outline

Online and in person

Courses run live online, so learners can join from anywhere, and in person at community venues. Every course ends with a showcase, where learners present what they've built.

Alongside the coding, we help learners write CVs, prepare for interviews and find their next step, whether that's a job, an apprenticeship or further study.

Where we're heading

In the future, we aim to bring our course to women in prison, with an offline version designed to work within prison education departments. Learning in prison helps people build skills and confidence for life after release.

If you work in prison education or resettlement and would like to talk, get in touch.

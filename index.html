Who our courses are for

Our courses are for anyone aged 16 or over who wants a way into tech and can't afford an expensive bootcamp. That includes people who are out of work, claiming Universal Credit, on a low income, or looking to change career.

You don't need any experience, qualifications or maths. You just need curiosity and a willingness to keep going when something doesn't work first time.

How it works

  1. Choose your course

    Six weeks, eight weeks or a full sixteen-week bootcamp, depending on your time and goals.

  2. Learn with a tutor

    Live sessions of one to two hours, online or in person, with help when you get stuck.

  3. Build real projects

    Websites, a game and a full web app, published online as your portfolio.

  4. Take the next step

    Help with your CV and interviews, and introductions to jobs, apprenticeships and further study.

What you'll learn

The same tools professional web developers use every day:

  • HTML and CSS to build and style web pages
  • JavaScript to make pages interactive
  • Python and Django to build full web apps with accounts and databases
  • Git and GitHub to save your work and share it with employers
